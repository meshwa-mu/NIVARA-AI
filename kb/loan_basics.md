# Loan basics in simple words
EMI means equated monthly instalment, the fixed amount paid every month to repay the loan with interest.

EMI coverage means monthly operating surplus divided by the EMI. A value above 1.5 gives a comfortable cushion, between 1.1 and 1.5 is thin, and below 1.1 means repayment may be difficult. These thresholds are used by Nivara as a rule of thumb, not an official bank rule.

Operating surplus is monthly revenue minus operating costs such as raw material, labour, electricity, transport and rent, before paying the EMI.

Working capital is the money needed for day to day running such as stock, feed or raw material. A business plan should keep some working capital aside in addition to equipment cost.

Nivara supports decisions only. It cannot guarantee that a business will succeed or that a loan will be sanctioned.
