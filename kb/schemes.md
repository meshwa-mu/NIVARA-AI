# Financing scheme terms (STARTER - replace with official scheme documents)
Source note: taken from the SIH26091 problem statement. Verify against the official scheme guidelines before relying on it.

Margin contribution: the entrepreneur typically contributes about 10% of the project cost and the channelizing agency finances about 90% through concessional finance. Project cost is therefore margin money divided by 0.10.

Micro Finance Scheme: applies when the project cost is up to Rs 1.40 lakh. Interest 6.5% per year, tenure 3 years, moratorium 3 months.

Term Loan Scheme: applies when the project cost is above Rs 1.40 lakh and up to Rs 50 lakh. Interest 8% per year, tenure 7 years, moratorium 6 months.

Moratorium: an initial period of the loan during which the repayment schedule is eased as per scheme rules. The exact treatment of interest during the moratorium must be confirmed in the official scheme document.
